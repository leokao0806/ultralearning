# Ultralearning Planner (Suite Redirect)

This skill is the legacy entry point for the **Ultralearning Skill Suite**.

To avoid superficial, generic multi-month plan dumps and ensure deep, iterative, stage-by-stage learning, this system is organized into 5 specialized modular skills:

1. **`using-ultralearning`** (Master orchestrator, anti-slop gatekeeper, and triage router)
2. **`metalearning`** (Phase 1: Deep motivation interview, syllabus benchmarking, CFP decomposition, 10% research budget)
3. **`sprint-planner`** (Phase 2: Granular 2-4 week sprint planning with strict pass/fail Gate Conditions)
4. **`drill-coach`** (Phase 3: Bottleneck isolation, 15-minute struggle timer, interactive Feynman test, deliberate drills)
5. **`audit-and-pivot`** (Phase 4: Weekly retrospective, rate-of-learning velocity check, diminishing returns triage, strategic experimentation)
